# Real Networkers LLC — Enterprise Network Design Project

Welcome to the official repository for **Real Networkers LLC**, established for our collaborative group project in **CIST 1401: Computer Networking Fundamentals**. 

Our mission is to design a robust, secure, and highly scalable enterprise-grade network infrastructure that satisfies our corporate client’s detailed technical requirements. In doing so, we aim to put the physical and logical networking principles we learn in this course—such as structured cabling, subnetting, TCP/IP protocols, and network segmentation—into active, real-world practice.

## 📜 Team Contract & Operating Charter

To maintain high accountability and ensure professional work ethics, our team operates under a formal **Real Networkers LLC Team Contract**. Below is a high-level summary of our governance, communication, and conflict-resolution standards:

### 1. Communication Protocols
*   **Primary Channel:** Microsoft Teams chat, supplemented by at least **one mandatory sync call per week** to review progress and delegate upcoming tasks.
*   **Secondary Channel:** Institutional email for sharing file attachments, draft revisions, and backup copies.
*   **Response SLA:** Every team member is expected to check the Teams channel at least **once every two days** and acknowledge read messages by "liking" them.

### 2. Democratic Decision-Making & Voting
Major design, architectural, and financial decisions are put to a democratic vote on Microsoft Teams using a structured emoji system to ensure clarity:
*   ❤️ **Heart:** Indifferent / Neutral
*   😆 **Laugh:** Approve / Support
*   😮 **Surprised:** Against / Disapprove

### 3. Accountability & Conflict Management
*   **Technical Disputes:** If members disagree on a technical detail (e.g., subnet design or hardware vendor), each side will post a short, written argument in the Teams channel. The team will resolve the issue democratically by vote.
*   **Underperformance & Apathy:** We are committed to a "win together or fall together" ethos. If a member fails to fulfill their designated role without notice and significantly impacts team progress, their decision-making power will be temporarily suspended, tasks will be redistributed, and they will receive a corresponding evaluation of zero on peer reviews.
*   **Emergency Leave Policy:** To handle life's surprises professionally, team members must provide **at least 1 day of prior notice** in the Teams chat if they are temporarily unable to complete their tasks, outlining their reason and an expected return date.
